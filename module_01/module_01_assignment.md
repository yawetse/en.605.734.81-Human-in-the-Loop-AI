# Module 1 - One Page Reflection

This assignment requires you to complete a **one-page response** to a question of your choice. Choose the one that most interests you and develop a high-quality, thoughtful response. Do not attempt to answer all questions. The goal is to demonstrate your understanding of the module and that you have read the relevant literature. Each response should **include citation(s) and reference(s)** that show you have gone deeper than what has been presented in lecture.  Your one-page response should be **double-spaced**.  Include your name and question number  in the **header or a separate cover page**. Provide the references on a separate page.

Do NOT submit a 2+ page response.  In the words of Mark Twain, "I didn't have time to write a short letter, so I wrote a long one instead". It is often challenging to be succinct and more difficult to constrain your thoughts to a single page.  The goal here is to force you to really reflect on the material and create a concise, stand-alone, referenced response that demonstrates  your understanding.

Late work may not be graded resulting in a score of 0 point for that module.  If you have extenuating circumstances you must coordinate with the professor in advance.

**Question 1**: 

Choose a real-world system (for example: Wikipedia, Waze, Community Notes, Foldit, reCAPTCHA, OpenStreetMap, Duolingo, Stack Overflow, citizen science projects, or another example of your choosing).

Explain:

- Why this system qualifies as a socio-technical system.
- How humans contribute computational value.
- What aspects of the task remain difficult for computers alone

**Question 2**: 

Many successful social computing platforms create enormous societal benefits while also introducing risks related to misinformation, bias, privacy, manipulation, or harmful behavior. Choose an existing social computing platform or online community and identify one significant ethical or societal challenge facing the platform. Then propose **two specific design changes** that could reduce this risk while maintaining the usefulness of the platform. Justify your recommendations using principles of socio-technical systems and Responsible AI discussed in this module.

**Question 3**: 

Imagine you have been asked to design an AI system for one of the following domains:

- Healthcare
- Education
- Software development
- Transportation
- Scientific research
- Another domain of your choice

Rather than describing the AI itself, focus on **where humans should remain involved**.

Discuss:

- Which tasks should be performed by AI.
- Which tasks should remain under human control.
- Why this allocation creates a more effective and responsible socio-technical system.

Support your recommendations using concepts introduced in this module.

Ensure your responses demonstrate a knowledge of the lesson material. Submit your completed assignment in MS Word or PDF formats.
