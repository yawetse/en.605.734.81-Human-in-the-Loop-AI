# Module 1 - Readings

**Required**

1. Quinn, A. J., & Bederson, B. B. (2011, May). [<u>Human computation: a survey and taxonomy of a growing fieldLinks to an external site.</u>](http://alexquinn.org/papers/Human%20Computation,%20A%20Survey%20and%20Taxonomy%20of%20a%20Growing%20Field%20\(CHI%202011\).pdf). In Proceedings of the SIGCHI conference on human factors in computing systems (pp. 1403-1412). ACM.
   - Read the first 3+ pages, up to “Classification Dimensions”
2. Ackerman, M. S. (2000). [<u>The intellectual challenge of CSCW: the gap between social requirements and technical feasibilityLinks to an external site.</u>](http://web.eecs.umich.edu/~ackerm/pub/00a10/hci.final.pdf). Human-computer interaction, 15(2), 179-203.
   - Read first 4+ pages, up to “The Social-Technical Gap in Action”
   - **Note:** CSCW = computer supported cooperative work, which is the study of the use of technology to augment an organization’s social activity.
3. Zafar, M. B., Valera, I., Gomez Rodriguez, M., & Gummadi, K. P. (2017, April). [<u>Fairness beyond disparate treatment & disparate impact: Learning classification without disparate mistreatmentLinks to an external site.</u>](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.08452.pdf). In *Proceedings of the 26th international conference on world wide web* (pp. 1171-1180).
