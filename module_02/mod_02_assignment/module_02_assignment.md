# Module 2 - Assignment

**Designing a Human Computation System**

In this assignment, you will design a human computation system to solve a real-world problem that cannot currently be solved effectively by AI alone. Your goal is not to build the system, but to design its architecture using the principles discussed in this module. Choose **one** real-world problem (or propose one of your own with instructor approval). Write a **2–3 page design document** (double spaced).

 

Your paper should include the following:

- Define the problem (why is it difficult? Why can’t computers reliable solve it alone?)
- Describe the workflow (How is work divided? How are tasks assigned? Are workers independent? Is there collaboration?) Include a simple workflow diagram.
- How will you ensure quality? i.e. redundancy, majority vote, expert review. Explain your reasoning.
- Why would people participate? i.e. financial compensation, altruism, competition
- Discuss two ethical issues. i.e. bias, privacy, transparency
- What part of your proposed system would be the most difficult to design?

Example domains include:

- Disaster response
- Scientific research
- Medical diagnosis
- Accessibility
- Fact checking
- Wildlife monitoring
- Historical document transcription
- Cybersecurity
- Environmental monitoring
- Software engineering
