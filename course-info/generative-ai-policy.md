# Generative AI Policy

### **Course Policy**

#### **✅ Green: Liberal GenAI use is permitted**

Because developing proficiency with GenAI tools is a core learning outcome, students are expected to engage with these tools throughout the course. Additional guidance is provided below: 

- **GenAI may be used throughout the workflow.** However, AI is not to be used to replace critical thinking. Follow specific guidelines for each assignment.
- **Disclosure of GenAI use is required**. When AI is used, include the following as an appendix to the assignment submission: 


- 

1. A Reference using APA interview format.  \
   Example: *ChatGPT-o5. (Year, Month Day). Purpose of Interaction. (Student_Name, Interviewer)*
2. The prompt(s) used and the responses provided (include the conversation).  Follow specific guidelines for each assignment.  A link to the conversation meets this requirement. 

- **Student responsibility:** Students remain fully responsible for verifying accuracy, identifying bias, and ensuring the quality of final submissions. 

1. 


1) Assignments are designed to uncover the strengths and weaknesses of AI when used in support of Systems Engineering.  To accomplish this objective, they incorporate both AI research and student analysis/assessment. 
2) Students are responsible for clearly separating AI-generated content from students’ original thought/critical thinking in their submissions.   

Unauthorized use of GenAI tools constitutes a violation of academic integrity policies. If you have questions about permitted GenAI use, please contact your instructor. EP provides [<u>student guidance on responsible GenAI useLinks to an external site.</u>](https://ep.jhu.edu/student-services/academic-services/academic-policies/student-guidance-on-responsible-generative-ai-use/).
